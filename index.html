FAQ

Got Questions?

We've answered the most common ones below. Still unsure? Give us a call — we're always happy to talk trees.

(985) 555-0128
Are you licensed and insured in Louisiana?

Absolutely. We are fully licensed to operate in Louisiana and carry comprehensive liability and worker's compensation insurance. We are happy to provide proof of insurance with every quote so you can have total peace of mind.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency storm services?

Yes. Bayou weather is unpredictable. If a storm knocks a tree onto your home, driveway, or power lines, you can call our emergency line day or night. We prioritize hazardous situations to get your family safe and your property secured immediately.

What's the difference between stump grinding and stump removal?

Stump grinding shreds the stump below soil level — it's faster, more affordable, and leaves you with usable mulch. Stump removal pulls the entire root ball out of the ground. We typically recommend grinding for residential yards as it's far less invasive.

How often should I have my trees trimmed?

For most mature trees in Houma, we recommend an inspection and trim every 3–5 years. Fast-growing species or trees near your roofline may need attention every 18–24 months, especially before hurricane season.

Will heavy machinery ruin my lawn?

We treat your property like it's our own. We use specialized mats to distribute equipment weight and protect your grass from ruts. Our "leave no trace" cleanup policy means we rake, blow, and haul away all debris — your yard will look better when we leave.

Do I need to be home during the job?

Not necessarily. As long as we have clear access to the yard (unlocked gates, no loose pets), our crew can complete the job while you're at work. We'll send photos of the completed job and can process payment over the phone for your convenience.

Can you help with insurance claims after storm damage?

Yes. We provide detailed photos and itemized invoices to help streamline your claim process. While we can't speak to your adjuster, we give you everything you need to get reimbursed quickly and with minimal hassle.
